# PiqX — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
**Idea — Match point.** The X of PiqX is the "vs" of every fixture and the × of every scoreline. The ball cut into its crossing is the live game. One mark that covers both halves of the product: scores and streams.

| Version | Use it for | Files |
|---|---|---|
| **Wordmark** (primary) | App header, splash, website, anything with room for the name | `svg/piqx-wordmark-*.svg` |
| **Stacked** (wordmark + tagline) | Splash, posters, social banners — 160 px wide or larger | `svg/piqx-stacked-*.svg` |
| **Symbol** | App icon, avatars, favicon, watermark on streams | `svg/piqx-symbol-*.svg` |
| **Symbol, small cut** | Anything 32 px or smaller (heavier arms, wider gap, bigger ball) | `svg/piqx-symbol-small-*.svg` |

The symbol *is* the last letter of the wordmark, so never place the symbol next to the wordmark — that would read "X PiqX".

Each version comes in four colourways: `on-dark` (default — the app is dark-first), `on-light`, `white`, `black`.
`*-tight.svg` files are cropped to the ink, with no clear space, for use inside layouts that already provide spacing.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **1 ring** on every side, where 1 ring = the diameter of the circle cut from the X (≈ 0.43 × the cap height of "P"). The standard SVGs already include this zone; the `-tight` versions do not.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | 24 px tall (≈ 52 px wide) | 12 mm wide |
| Stacked | 160 px wide | 35 mm wide |
| Symbol | 33 px+ standard; 16–32 px use the small cut | 5 mm (small cut) |

## 4. Colour
| Name | Role | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone (closest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electric Green | X arms (on dark) | `#00F59B` | 0 245 155 | 60 0 55 0 | 3395 C ¹ |
| Signal Cyan | Ball (on dark) | `#00D2FF` | 0 210 255 | 70 0 0 0 | 306 C ¹ |
| Night | Backgrounds, text on light | `#111318` | 17 19 24 | 75 65 55 80 | Black 6 C |
| Mist | Text on dark | `#E2E2E9` | 226 226 233 | 10 8 4 0 | Cool Gray 1 C |
| Deep Green | X arms (on light) | `#00965E` | 0 150 94 | 99 0 84 0 | 340 C |
| Deep Cyan | Ball (on light) | `#007C99` | 0 124 153 | 100 20 20 30 | 3145 C |

¹ The screen greens and cyans are brighter than any standard ink. Proof against a printed swatch before a print run.

These are the same tokens as `lib/theme/app_theme.dart`. **Signal red (`#FF3B30`) is reserved for LIVE badges** — never use it in the logo.

**Approved pairs:** on-dark colours on Night (default) · on-light colours on white or `#F4F6F9` · white on Night, Deep Green or photos · black on white.
Bright Electric Green on white fails contrast — use the on-light version there.

## 5. Typography
- Display / headlines: **Space Grotesk** 600–700, tight tracking (−1 to −3 %). "Piq" in the wordmark is outlined Space Grotesk Bold.
- Body: **Plus Jakarta Sans** 400–700.
- Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License and bundled in `assets/fonts/`.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't retype the wordmark in a font — use the files · don't put the symbol beside the wordmark · don't recolour outside the palette or make the ball red · don't stretch, rotate or skew · don't remove the gap around the ball or fill it in · don't add shadows, outlines or gradients to the mark (glows behind it, as on the splash screen, are fine) · don't place the colour logo on busy photos — use white on a calm area.

## 8. Rebuilding
All SVG masters are generated by `source/build_logo.py` (needs `pip install fonttools`). PNGs were rendered from those SVGs; re-render after any change.
Before registering the mark, get a professional trademark search — no clearance has been done.
